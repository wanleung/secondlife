Samsara Life Map

Every road in the game: how one life runs from birth to the Bridge of Helplessness, how the eleven extraordinary careers rise, the Freemasonry side path, how politics works under eight kinds of regime, faiths and their doors out of the cycle, how money is made, how to study, work, settle and naturalise abroad, and the ordinary jobs most people do.

How a life runs

Each stage of life draws events. After death, King Yama’s court tallies merit and sin into karma, and karma decides your next life.

Regimes

Each country’s regime comes from V-Dem’s Regimes of the World, 2025: 0 closed autocracy, 1 electoral autocracy, 2 electoral democracy, 3 liberal democracy. Closed autocracies are further split into one-party, monarchy, junta, theocracy and so on as a game setting. Your political path follows the regime of the country you live in.

Money

In US dollars. Each country’s income per person is the World Bank’s real GNI per capita (Taiwan and North Korea are estimates), so the same job can pay dozens of times more in one country than another.

Annual income = income per person in your country of residence × job multiplier Savings each year ≈ income × savings rate, but living costs are at least 20% of income per person, so on very low pay you eat into savings. Savings rate by background: destitute 0%, poor 3%, working class 8%, middle class 12%, wealthy 25%, elite 35%; university +5%; marriage and children reduce it; migrant workers in rich countries save an extra 25% to send home. Family wealth at birth: destitute 0, poor 0.05, working class 0.3, middle class 1.5, wealthy 8, elite 60 × income per person. Retire at 65: rich countries pay a pension (30% of income per person); elsewhere only 5%.
